Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Two Hearts ("the app", "we", "us") is a twin pregnancy companion app for expectant mothers and their partners. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is stored, and how it is used.

1. What data we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and your role (mama or partner).
  • Pregnancy data: estimated due date, current week, pre-pregnancy weight, and weight log entries.
  • Health & wellness data: symptom logs, supplement tracking, visit notes, and appointment details.
  • Baby data: ultrasound scan images (stored on your device only), and fetal measurements you enter manually.
  • Journal entries: free-text notes and symptom entries you write within the app.
  • Contacts: midwife or care provider details you enter.
  • Push notification tokens: a device-specific token used only to send you pregnancy-related reminders and partner alerts.

2. How data is stored

  • All shared pregnancy data (appointments, symptoms, notes, weight, etc.) is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database secured by Firebase Authentication. Only you and your linked partner can access your pregnancy data.
  • Ultrasound scan images are stored locally on your device only using encrypted device storage. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • Push notification tokens are stored in Firebase alongside your pregnancy data and are only used to deliver notifications between you and your partner.

3. Who can see your data

  • You and your linked partner — pregnancy data is shared between both partners via a secure invite code you generate.
  • No one else. We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties.

4. AI features

Some features (pregnancy chat, weekly briefings, and visit note structuring) send text to the Anthropic Claude API to generate responses. Messages sent to this feature are processed by Anthropic's servers. Please do not share sensitive personal information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

The app may send you local reminders (supplement alerts, appointment reminders) and push notifications from your partner (new appointments, symptom logs, scan saves). You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained in Firebase for as long as your account exists. To delete your data:

  • Contact us at dylan.ndengu@gmail.com and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
  • Scan images stored on your device can be deleted from within the app's Scans screen.

7. Children's privacy

Two Hearts is intended for adults (expectant parents). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at dylan.ndengu@gmail.com.
